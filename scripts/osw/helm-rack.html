The Helm Rack

Every piece of face-covering headgear in the OpenSourceWeb art, 99 of them, drawn at 3x. A thing is on this rack only if wearing it hides who you are — open helmets, caps, crowns and wigs are not here. Files live in assets/osw-helms/. Click one to see it big.

The piped ones

If what you want is the masked, tubed, faintly technological warrior — the HighFleet look, a mask with a hose running off it — start here. These six are the whole of it in this set.

Where these come from. helms/, masks/ and hoods/ are copies out of assets/osw-sprites/, originally icons/obj/clothing/{hats,masks}.dmi. ceremonial/ is new to the repo: it comes from icons/life/masks.dmi, a Lifeweb sheet the main extractor never walks. Nothing is modified — each file is the frame exactly as it came out of the sheet, south-facing only.
