Ravenheart Sound Board

A board of 237 sounds, one from each family, out of the 662 exported from OpenSourceWeb into assets/osw-sounds/ and grouped by what a sound is for. Click one to hear it. The whole export is in the repo; this plays the pick of it.

Where these come from. OpenSourceWeb, the open-licensed archive of the BYOND game Lifeweb / Farweb. Its icons and sounds are CC BY-SA 3.0; the code is not, and none of it is here. Source: SS13-Special-Codebases-Archive/OpenSourceWeb.

This board carries 237 of the 662 exported sounds — one from each family, so slash1 is here and slash2 through slash5 are not, and the music is five tracks rather than eight. All 662 are in assets/osw-sounds/. The full source tree holds 3,559 files and 484 MB, most of it unusable here: 616 vox words, 170 MIDI files, the piano and violin note banks, 229 MB of jukebox music. To pull back something the export left behind, edit the lists in scripts/osw/export-sounds.py and run it again.
