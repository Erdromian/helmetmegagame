--- MAIN MESSAGE ---

Bascinet is a large-scale, rules-light story generator, 60% roleplay and 40% strategy, set in an ethereal barony below the barren surface of the planet Evergreen. It's intended to be serious, grim, heroic, and occasionally funny.

The game runs for several weekly sessions, anywhere from 10 to 30 days depending on the game. Bascinet's main system is Gambits, moves that consume your turn. When you make Gambits, you roll a die, and a Gamemaster adjudicates the situation depending on your character's Tags.

--- THREAD: Rules ---

① **Basic** — You must be 18+.
-Erotic roleplay is not allowed, however romance is.
-Contemporary slurs and hatespeech have no place in Bascinet. Bigotry is tolerated so long as its purpose is to forward the story and drive conflict between characters, use your head and you'll be fine.
-Hostility should only be between people's in game characters. Bascinet contains many infuriating characters, channeling that frustration towards being toxic to your fellow players is not acceptable.

② **IC Rules** — Anything that can be reasonably justified is perfectly acceptable.
- The only rule is to remain in character. As long as what you are doing is reasonable for your character, you are free to do whatever you wish.

③ **Information** — To support information asymmetry, IC and OOC should be separated.
- Do not discuss anything relating to an ongoing game.
- Try not to spoil secret mechanics for new players. Learning is the fun part, and should not be robbed from someone.
- Don't say who your character is or who you're playing.

**Lore**

Humanity retreated into the caves after the Long Night. Evergreen was once beautiful and lush, but for unknown reasons, the surface froze and became barren, destroying its ecosystems.

Far from the sun, electric lights illuminate Ravenheart, an ethereal castle ruled by its Baron. The only source of power is the Lifeweb — a revolutionary invention which turns human blood into electricity. While the nobility enjoy electrical appliances in the Keep and the merchant sells weird wares and lost technology, the life of a common Ravenhearter is medieval. Most of them use fire for light and simple tools for their work. They are superstitious and mostly illiterate. The Post-Christian Church is the official faith of Ravenheart, though some keep to the Old Ways in secret. Most of their days are spent working hard, but there is time for merry every once in a while. The current year is 1098.

--- THREAD: Turns ---

The game is synced to real time. You don't have to play 24/7; asynchronous play is better.

Every turn, you can take actions such as cooking, crafting, and traveling. However, you can also take a Gambit. Gambit moves are actions that could affect the narrative and could go any way. Fighting, trying to discover a bandit outpost, and stealing from the Inn are all Gambits. Gambits require a 1d6 roll and take your entire turn.

Like Mafia, Moves are collectively adjudicated by the Gamemasters at the end of every turn.

--- THREAD: Roleplay ---

Each zone has its own Locations. Walking to a connected Location inside your Zone is free, but crossing into another Zone uses your turn. Everyone has one free Zone move per turn.

Every surface zone has a summary channel: optionally describe, in broad terms, what other people in your Zone saw you doing. You can roleplay by using each Location's rooms.

You may not roleplay in DMs or outside of the server.

--- THREAD: Tags ---

Tags represent your character's health, items, traits, and skills.

The main way to obtain new Tags is to spend tag points. Everyone gains more through the game as they fulfill Desires. However, you can also create, inflict, and remove tags throughout the game — such as by smithing a sword, poisoning someone, or treating a wound.
