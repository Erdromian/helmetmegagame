--- MAIN MESSAGE ---

Bascinet is a large-scale, rules-light story generator, 70% roleplay and 30% strategy. It's intended to be serious, grim, heroic, and occasionally funny.

The game runs for 30 real days. Once a day, you declare what your character did, making a Move. Based on dice and your character's Tags, the GMs adjudicate the resulting consequences. Mechanics are handled on the companion web panel while the roleplay takes place on Discord.

Bascinet simultaneously plays out on several levels. Kings win wars and move big pieces around the map while commoners try to fulfill their day-to-day desires.

--- THREAD: Rules ---

① **Basic** — You must be 18+.
- Romance is allowed. You can hint at sexual content, but nothing overt (fade to black!).
- There are no children in Ravenheart.
- Mild bigotry is fine. SA and similar aren't.

② **Atmosphere** — Everything serves the atmosphere.
- Act somewhat like a Medieval European. Play out fear (unless you have the **Brave** tag). Ravenheart is your home, your life.
- Insulting the Baron would mean death, plus having your family ostracized. Think carefully.

③ **Information** — To support information asymmetry, everything that happens IC stays IC, and OOC stays OOC.
- Don't discuss the game's happenings OOC. Vague references are okay.
- Don't say who your character is or who you're playing. People might figure it out eventually, but protect the mystery.
- You are allowed to use vague OOC in roleplay channels when necessary — `(OOC: hey, gtg, see ya)` — instead of DM'ing the player.

④ **Antagonism** — Fulfill your character's Desires, but not to the point of wrecking the people your character answers to.
- High ranks are expected to be relatively fit for duty and willing to collaborate. Peasants can brawl and do mostly whatever they want.
- If things naturally progress to where you turn on your own, that's fine, but come into the game planning to do mostly good.

--- THREAD: Fates ---

Choose a fate at game start, determining your starting tags and location. Whitelisted roles (★) are reserved for players who can vouch frequent and long-term availability and proficiency in roleplay.

Most fates have small limits, but **Bolded** ones have higher caps and are more generalized, allowing for more freedom of play.

--- THREAD: Lore ---

**Lore**

Humanity retreated into the caves after the Long Night. Evergreen was once beautiful and lush, but for unknown reasons, the surface froze and became barren, destroying its ecosystems.

Far from the sun, electric lights illuminate Ravenheart, an ethereal castle ruled by its Baron. The only source of power is the Lifeweb — a revolutionary invention which turns human blood into electricity. While the nobility enjoy electrical appliances in the Keep and the merchant sells weird wares and lost technology, the life of a common Ravenhearter is medieval. Most of them use fire for light and simple tools for their work. They are superstitious and mostly illiterate. The Post-Christian Church is the official faith of Ravenheart, though some keep to the Old Ways in secret. Most of their days are spent working hard, but there is time for merry every once in a while. The current year is 1098.

**Is there magic?**
Probably not.

**Is there technology?**
Sort of; the rich have electricity and the merchant has some very weird goods, but peasants are straight out of the 11th century.

**Beliefs?**
Most people are Post-Christian, but some people believe in the Old Ways.

**Species?**
Everyone's a human (?).

--- CATEGORY: Gameplay ---

Bascinet has two layers: the Move layer (strategic) and the Roleplay layer (narrative). Moves are adjudicated once a turn, and are where mechanical effects take place. The Roleplay layer is always going on, and takes the results of the Move layer as its canon.

--- THREAD: Moves ---

A turn is 6, 8, 12 or 24 hours long — the GMs set which — and always ends on a clean hour, US Central. That is the game's clock, and every deadline you are given is in it. Every turn, you can optionally plan a Move that'll take effect the next turn.

Moves are your abstracted intent of what you planned to do over the turn. Moves are reserved for lengthy, time-consuming, or difficult activities.

**Examples**:
- robbing someone;
- cooking several meals;
- collecting taxes around the town;
- going to the inn to fulfill a Desire.

*You don't have to Move every turn*. If it didn't take more than an hour or wasn't difficult, it's not a Move. Bandaging people, trading, and talking are not Moves.

Like Mafia, Moves are collectively adjudicated by the Gamemasters at the end of every turn. If the Baron's move was to stay in his castle, then the Brigand's move to rob him won't succeed. If the Baron was wandering in the forest, the Brigand may very well get him.

Adjudication results are messaged to players at the end of every turn.

**Routine / Gambit**
There are two types of Moves: **Routine** and **Gambit**.

Routine moves have no chance to fail — their results are usually just accepted and canonized at turn end. Hunting, farming, going out for drinks, or smithing are all Routine.

Gambit moves are actions that could affect the narrative and could go any way. Fighting, trying to discover a bandit outpost, and stealing from the Inn are all Gambits. Gambits require a 1d6 roll.

**Group Moves**
Several characters can act together as one Move, with everyone who agreed to take part written into it. Their tags are considered during the adjudication. Only one Gambit die is rolled.

--- THREAD: Roleplay ---

The game's roleplay layer is where the results of last turn's Moves come to life. If the Gamemaster declared you were bested in a duel, that's canon; you can describe how it went down in the roleplay channels. You can also use the roleplay channels at any time to chat, exchange information, or coordinate your Moves with other players.

**Channels and Zones**
There are five surface zones. Each has their own Locations. Walking to a connected Location inside your Zone is free, but crossing into another Zone uses your turn. Everyone has one free Zone move per turn.

Every surface zone has a summary channel: optionally describe, in broad terms, what other people in your Zone saw you doing. You can roleplay by using each Location's rooms.

Every Location has its own channel — the only one you see. Talk in it, step into one of its public Rooms (the threads under it), or use the pinned message's buttons: **Who's here?**, **Secret rooms?** (the private Rooms your tags let you into), and **Converse** (a private thread; then `/add (character)` to invite people).

You cannot roleplay in DMs or outside of the server.

**Formatting**
Here's the recommended way to write a post:
- `"text"` for dialogue.
- text for emotes or description.
- `(text)` for out-of-character text.

You can append `-#` before your OOC text to make it tiny and hidden, or even spoiler it with `||text||`.

--- THREAD: Resources ---

The game's central currency is Resources (⬢), abstracting food, money, and other bulk goods.

Players are responsible for producing, taxing, distributing, and trading Resources.

Characters automatically consume 1 ⬢ a day. If you go hungry, you'll get a −1 to your Gambit. The penalty increases until you die at −6.

--- THREAD: Tags ---

Tags represent your character's health, items, traits, and skills.

The main way to obtain new Tags is to spend Tag Points. Everyone starts with Tag Points but gain more through the game as they fulfill Desires. However, you can also create, inflict, and remove tags throughout the game — such as by smithing a sword, poisoning someone, or treating a wound.
