--- MAIN MESSAGE ---

Bascinet is a large-scale, rules-light story generator, 60% roleplay and 40% strategy, set in an ethereal barony below the barren surface of the planet Evergreen. It's intended to be serious, grim, heroic, and occasionally funny.

The game runs for several weekly sessions, anywhere from 10 to 30 days depending on the game. Bascinet's main system is Gambits, moves that consume your turn. When you make Gambits, you roll a die, and a Gamemaster adjudicates the situation depending on your character's Tags.

--- THREAD: Rules ---

① **Basic** — You must be 18+.
- Erotic roleplay is not allowed, however romance is.
- Contemporary slurs and hatespeech have no place in Bascinet. Bigotry is tolerated so long as its purpose is to forward the story and drive conflict between characters, use your head and you'll be fine.
- Hostility should only be between people's in game characters. Bascinet contains many infuriating characters, channeling that frustration towards being toxic to your fellow players is not acceptable.

② **IC Rules** — Anything that can be reasonably justified is perfectly acceptable.
- The only rule is to remain in character. As long as what you are doing is reasonable for your character, you are free to do whatever you wish.

③ **Information** — To support information asymmetry, IC and OOC should be separated.
- Do not discuss anything relating to an ongoing game.
- Try not to spoil secret mechanics for new players. Learning is the fun part, and should not be robbed from someone.
- Don't say who your character is or who you're playing.

--- THREAD: Fates ---

Choose a fate at game start, determining your starting tags and location. Whitelisted roles (★) are reserved for players who demonstrate availability and a high commitment to preserving the atmosphere.

--- THREAD: Lore ---

**Evergreen**

We are on Evergreen. A distant planet from humanity's origin of Earth, it has for centuries been shrouded in darkness and isolated. As the sun-deprived surface froze over, almost all of humanity upon Evergreen moved underground. In a state of planetary stasis humanity has regressed to a neo-feudal state, only that which benefits those in power has been maintained and preserved.

Under the steady hand of the God-King, Evergreen has been shielded from the horrors of the cosmos, but all things must come to an end one way or another.

**God-King**

"The God-King watches over all. One eye turned to the soil below, one to the skies above. You don't need to understand his will. In fact, I'm here because I don't expect you to." — A Tribunal officer, visiting Ravenheart

The God-King Enoch is the absolute monarch of Evergreen. He has ruled for centuries and no memory of the world before his rise to power is left in the public consciousness. He rules from the planet's capital, Gahan. The idea of opposing him is near unthinkable, it would be as if to oppose Evergreen itself.

The title of God-King was appointed to him long ago by the church itself, when the reality dawned that he was no mere mortal king. The church and the God-King exist in a symbiotic relationship, both feeding power and legitimacy to the other and centralizing the planet around themselves. While he is not considered an embodiment of God-in-Coma, his absolute rule grants him a prophet-like status and unending devotion from the faithful.

**Post-Christianity**

God has surely not abandoned us; he merely slumbers in deep restless sleep.

The Grey Postchristian Church (GPC) is the only legally recognized religion on Evergreen. For centuries it has served as the planet's moral compass, guiding it through its blind and confused isolation.

Postchristians believe that God-in-Coma is trapped in an eternal nightmare, and when he wakes, he will vanquish the horrors of the cosmos and unveil a new age of paradise. For this to happen, the dark forces binding him must be driven from this world, this task falling to the Church and Inquisition.

Postchristianity bears almost no resemblance to real life Christianity beyond the general appearances. The doctrine calls for mercy on the holy and suffering for the wicked. Any who openly defy the church are labelled heretics and purged, lest they damn us all.

**Ravenheart**

Ravenheart is the fortress in which the game takes place, a minor settlement located in the north of Evergreen. Buried deep below the surface ruled by the Baron it offers some semblance of refuge amongst the hostile caves that surround it. All other sources of energy exhausted long ago, the Lifeweb remains as Ravenheart's only source of electricity, feeding on the very lifeforce of the population.

Based on its isolation, Ravenheart has significant autonomy from the rest of the God-King's domain. The Baron is still by law a vassal to both the Duke, and the God-King himself, but rarely do either interfere with the goings on of the fortress.

The current year is 1098. The fortress still glows in the dark, damp caves, but it will wink out like a candle should the Lifeweb cease to sustain it.

--- THREAD: Turns ---

The game is synced to real time. You don't have to play 24/7; asynchronous play is better.

Every turn, you can take actions such as cooking, crafting, and traveling. However, you can also take a Gambit. Gambit moves are actions that could affect the narrative and could go any way. Fighting, trying to discover a bandit outpost, and stealing from the Inn are all Gambits. Gambits require a 1d6 roll and take your entire turn.

Like Mafia, Moves are collectively adjudicated by the Gamemasters at the end of every turn.

--- THREAD: Roleplay ---

Each zone has its own Locations. Walking to a connected Location inside your Zone is free, but crossing into another Zone uses your turn. Everyone has one free Zone move per turn.

Every surface zone has a summary channel: optionally describe, in broad terms, what other people in your Zone saw you doing. You can roleplay by using each Location's rooms.

You may not roleplay in DMs or outside of the server.

--- THREAD: Tags ---

Tags represent your character's health, items, traits, and skills.

The main way to obtain new Tags is to spend tag points. Everyone gains more through the game as they fulfill Desires. However, you can also create, inflict, and remove tags throughout the game — such as by smithing a sword, poisoning someone, or treating a wound.
